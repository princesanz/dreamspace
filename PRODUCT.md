# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, potential clients, and fellow creative developers visiting Gilang Afrisda's portfolio. Desktop-first audience arriving from a link (CV, social, referral) expecting to judge craft within seconds. Mobile visitors get a functional, lighter ride. Reduced-motion users get an equivalent journey without continuous flight.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio where the medium is the proof: instead of listing "I do WebGL and motion," the site *is* WebGL and motion. The visitor flies through a foggy void along a curved camera path; sections are stations in space. Success = the visitor reaches the contact gate, remembers the experience, and can still read/scan everything (projects, skills, contact) without friction.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, weightless, alive. "Sci-fi film trailer, not a website" (spec §2). Bold and maximal by explicit owner mandate — Sanz rejects quiet/minimal/derivative directions. Copy voice: Indonesian, conversational-cosmic, plain verbs, the flight metaphor carried consistently (terbang, stasiun, monolit, gerbang).

## Anti-references

- Conventional clean/minimal portfolio templates (flat hero + card grid + footer).
- Static pages that happen to have a 3D background — the 3D world must BE the navigation.
- Anything that looks frozen when idle.
- The generic AI landing page (cream bg / serif display / terracotta accent).

## Design Principles

1. **The medium is the proof** — every capability claimed is demonstrated by the site itself.
2. **Nothing stands still** — every object rotates, drifts, pulses, or reacts; idle ≠ frozen.
3. **Bold with receipts** — maximal visuals, but AA text contrast, keyboard nav, and reduced-motion parity are non-negotiable (spec §8).
4. **The journey is the information architecture** — scroll = flight; stations = sections; progress rail = map.
5. **60fps is a feature** — adaptive quality degrades effects before it degrades motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA text contrast on all overlay copy (#8f88b8 on #030308 ≈ 6.3:1 ✓). `prefers-reduced-motion` → no continuous flight, eased station transitions, fade sections. Full keyboard navigation (arrow/page keys between stations, Escape closes panels, visible focus). Semantic HTML for all overlay content. Touch: native scrolling, tap interactions, reduced particles.
